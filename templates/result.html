How to interpret these explanations

A positive SHAP contribution pushes the model's prediction higher relative to its baseline, while a negative contribution pushes it lower. The magnitude indicates the contribution in the model's output units. These explanations describe model behavior; they do not establish that a feature causes a particular academic outcome.
